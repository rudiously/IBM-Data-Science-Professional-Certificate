# निवांत ट्रेकर्स - खलिता शैलीतील वेबसाइट 🏔️📜

A unique trekking website designed as a traditional Marathi "Khalita" (royal letter) with aged parchment aesthetics, Devanagari calligraphy, and cultural heritage design.

## विशेषता (Features) ✨

- **खलिता शैली (Khalita Style)**: Traditional Marathi royal letter format
- **पार्चमेंट डिझाइन**: Aged paper texture with ink stains and fold marks
- **मोहीम (Missions)**: Trek presented as historical missions
- **युद्धयोजना (Battle Plan)**: Itinerary styled as strategic planning
- **मराठी भाषा**: Primary language with cultural authenticity
- **व्यवस्थापन पॅनेल**: Full admin control over all content
- **Responsive**: Works on all devices

## Design Elements 🎨

### Visual Style
- Aged parchment/old paper texture background
- Saffron (केसरी), deep maroon, ink black, stone grey colors
- Handwritten calligraphy-style Devanagari fonts
- Wax seal graphic with "श्री" symbol
- Rough paper edges, ink stains, fold marks
- Subtle dust and paper animations

### Typography
- **Primary**: Tiro Devanagari Marathi (Marathi text)
- **Secondary**: IM Fell English (English accents)
- **Headings**: Cinzel (decorative)

### Cultural Elements
- Royal letter greeting: "प्रति, सह्याद्रीप्रेमी वीर,"
- Emotional, historical content tone
- Signature: "— निवांत ट्रेकर्स"
- Closing: "जय भवानी, जय शिवाजी"

## Quick Start 🚀

1. Open `index.html` - View the Khalita (letter homepage)
2. Click "✍️ मोहिमेत सहभागी व्हा" - See trek details
3. Open `admin.html` - Add/edit all content in Marathi

## Website Structure 📜

### खलिता (Front of Letter - index.html)
- Title: "खलिता"
- Subtitle: "सह्याद्री मोहिमेसाठी"
- Wax seal at top
- Opening address to "सह्याद्रीप्रेमी वीर"
- Emotional letter body about Sahyadri forts
- Mission cards showing treks
- Call-to-action button
- Royal signature

### मोहीम तपशील (Trek Details - trek.html)
- दुर्ग विवरण (Fort Details)
- ऐतिहासिक महत्व (Historical Significance)
- चित्र संग्रह (Image Gallery)
- पुणे प्रवास बिंदू (Pune Pickup Points)
- युद्धयोजना (Battle Plan/Itinerary)
- खर्च (Fees)
- समावेश (Inclusions)
- आवश्यक साहित्य (Things to Carry)
- महत्वाच्या सूचना (Important Instructions)
- संपर्क व नोंदणी (Contact & Booking)

### व्यवस्थापन पॅनेल (Admin Panel - admin.html)
Full control over:
- Trek name, route, difficulty (in Marathi)
- Hero background & gallery images
- Fort details & history
- Pickup points & itinerary
- Fees, inclusions, carry items
- Instructions & contacts
- Instagram handle

## Design Philosophy 🏰

This is NOT a modern corporate website. It's designed to feel like:
- Receiving a personal letter from the mountains
- A royal call to adventure in Sahyadri
- Historical fort records and documents
- Shivaji Maharaj's letters (respectful, inspiring)
- Premium scrapbook/travel journal aesthetics

## Focus Area 📍

- **Location**: Pune & Sahyadri forts only
- **Culture**: Marathi heritage and history
- **Emotion**: Connection to roots and ancestors
- **Quality**: Canva-premium level aesthetics
